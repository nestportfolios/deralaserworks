OUR CORE SERVICES

What We Do Best

From full building facade upgrades to precision-cut illuminated shop signs, our in-house team handles design, fabrication, and mounting.

3D Illuminated Sign Boards

Custom 3D channel letters made with high-grade acrylic and stainless steel. Available in front-lit, halo-backlit, and edge-lit designs with weather-sealed LED modules for long-lasting nighttime brightness.

  • Cast acrylic faces (anti-yellowing)
  • Stainless steel & metal returns
  • Waterproof Samsung/Osram LEDs

Architectural ACP Cladding

Exterior and interior Aluminum Composite Panel (ACP) cladding for plazas, commercial store fronts, and building elevations. Gives your building a clean, modern look while protecting it from weather.

  • 4mm PVDF coated panels
  • Galvanized iron sub-frame structure
  • Silicone expansion weather seals

CNC & Fiber Laser Cutting

High-accuracy cutting of stainless steel, mild steel, brass, and acrylic. Ideal for decorative architectural jali screens, boundary partitions, custom emblems, and indoor branding.

  • Clean, burr-free edges
  • Custom geometric & Islamic patterns
  • Up to heavy-gauge metal sheets
STEP-BY-STEP

How We Deliver Your Project

We handle every detail from the initial site measurement to final electrical hookup.

1

Site Measurement

We inspect the building, take exact laser measurements, and check electrical connection points.

2

3D Design & Approval

You receive a clear 3D preview of the signboard or cladding on your building before work begins.

3

Workshop Fabrication

We cut, bend, and assemble using CNC lasers, genuine cast acrylic, and waterproof LED modules.

4

Mounting & Testing

Our rigging team installs the framing, mounts the panels/letters, and completes full nighttime lighting tests.
